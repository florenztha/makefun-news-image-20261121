1KYIV, UKRAINE

Russia fires 510 weapons at Ukraine; missiles land in Poland

More than 400 drones and 110 missiles hit Ukraine overnight. At least 8 people were killed in Kyiv, bridges and stations damaged, and about 20 more wounded in Kryvyi Rih. Two missiles and a drone came down in Poland; Tusk called it a deliberate provocation.

2SHENZHEN, CHINA

Trump, Xi and Putin sign a peace declaration that skips Ukraine

The US, Chinese and Russian leaders met three ways for the first time and issued a joint peace declaration, with Xi at centre stage. The text said nothing about Ukraine, and afterwards Trump told European leaders to defund Kyiv.

3THE HAGUE, NETHERLANDS

World court: Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya

The Court of Justice found Myanmar responsible for genocide against the Rohingya, the first time an international court has found a state guilty of genocide. Myanmar called it unfair.

4NEW YORK, UNITED STATES

AI rout tips Wall Street into correction

US stocks fell 3.7% as an AI and chip sell-off pushed the S&P 500 and Dow into correction, the same week Anthropic opened trading worth about $2 trillion.

5ANTALYA, TURKEY

COP31 runs into overtime over fossil fuels

Nearly 200 nations at the UN climate summit failed to agree by the deadline, deadlocked over fossil fuel language that environmentalists say the draft ignores.

6BRUSSELS, BELGIUM

Britain and the EU sign their biggest post-Brexit reset

PM Burnham and EU leaders signed three agreements in Brussels on food exports, carbon markets and youth mobility, a decade after the Brexit vote.

7LONDON, UNITED KINGDOM

Pill makes pancreatic tumours vanish in 1 in 12 patients

In a trial of the pill daraxonrasib, scans found no detectable cancer in one in 12 patients, a landmark result against one of the deadliest cancers.
